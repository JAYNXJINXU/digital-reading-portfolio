## Title of Reading Selection
    `Student Perceptions of ChatGPT Use in a College Essay Assignment: Implications for Learning, Grading, and Trust in Artificial Intelligence`

<br>

<h5>
The paper presents a convincing thesis in which ChatGPT turns the student writing experience into active collaboration that requires human oversight and
trust, rather than simply a cheating tool. I strongly stand with this perspective as AI must be used only as a tool for assistance not a tool with absolute authority.
The authors were able to achieve their text’s purpose through the well-structured pre- and post-study design on the undergraduate students. The clarity of the
paper excels in carefully explaining its methodology and thematic analysis, making the research accessible. The logic of the study is strong as the researchers
systematically compare the student’s anticipated assignment difficulty with their actual experiences with AI. The paper is also strictly objective as their
conclusions are grounded in quantifiable data collected through the likert scale surveys and observed frequencies. The paper has openly documented the negative
outcomes, especially the occasional poor accuray and limited feedback. The logical progression of the study’s findings are also strengthened by the included
organized visual data. The highlighted findings of students still overwhelmingly choose human oversight for grading rather than relying solely on AI, highlights
the authors decision on not blindly praising technology. To conclude, this paper is clear and logical enough to serve as a blueprint on how students can ethically
integrate AI into their coursework.
</h5>

## Title of Reading Selection
    `What’s next in AI: 7 trends to watch in 2026`

<br>

<h5>
The article’s core argument, which is the transition of AI from being a simple tool to a collaborative partner across various industries, offers a grounded
and encouraging outlook for the modern workplace. We agree with this viewpoint, as framing AI as a digital teammate creates an environment where it encourages
individuals to sharpen their own problem-solving and creative skills rather than completely relying on automation. Sussana Ray strengthens this argument by
explaining how the partnership could affect the work and research through an example in which she described a small three person team using AI to handle the
data and generate content while people direct the strategy and creativity. We find this example convincing because it shows which task AI could handle and which
task would remain to be done by people. Although Ray presents an optimistic vision of the AI's potential to accelerate human productivity and discovery, the
article emphasizes autonomous “digital coworkers," which is an urgent need for strict human oversight to prevent overreliance and systemic risk. This article
addresses this concern through discussion of AI security, explaining that AI agents should have clear identities, limited access to information, and proper protection
against possible threats. We also find the potential use of AI in healthcare, as it could help improve access to medical services and assist with tasks such as
diagnosing conditions and planning treatments. The article's separate sections and clear examples make its seven predictions quite easy to follow, even for readers
unfamiliar with AI. However, relying only on the Microsoft experts limits its objectivity, and including independent opinions would make its claim a bit more
balanced. Overall, Ray succeeded in explaining how AI could support human work, but we believe these benefits depend on careful use and continued human
judgement or oversight. 
</h5>
